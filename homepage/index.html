问题是——你没法核对它。我们想让每一句话，都能被追到它的原文。

Most AI-written long reads sound convincing. The trouble is, you can’t check them. We want every sentence to lead back to the passage it came from.

分头写，再交叉审Write in parallel, then cross-review

多个子 agent 按小节并行写作，每句只能引用库里有的段落，编造的段号会被丢弃。写完换一组视角审查，偏薄、越界、没答上的地方返修；返修也补不上的，写进「存疑与未完成」。Sub-agents write sections in parallel and may only cite passages in the library — fabricated citations are dropped. A separate reviewer then flags thin, out-of-scope or unanswered parts for revision; what still can’t be fixed goes into “Open questions”.

02证据Evidence

能被核对的，
才算研究If you can’t check it,
it isn’t research

差别不在能不能写长文，而在写出来的东西，能不能对上原文。以下都取自同一次真实运行。The difference isn’t whether it can write long — it’s whether what it writes holds up against the source. Everything below comes from the same real run.

引用Citation

引用挂在句子上，
不堆在文末Citations live on the sentence,
not in a footer

每个判断后面挂一个段号，指向原文里具体的那一段。点开就能看到它引了什么、来自哪篇、属于哪类材料。Every claim carries a passage number pointing at one specific paragraph of the source. Open it to see exactly what was quoted, from where, and what kind of source it is.

一份报告，200 处引用，指向 49 段不同的原文。One report: 200 citations pointing at 49 distinct passages.

来源Sources

来源先分性质，
再当证据用Sources are graded
before they’re used

每个来源被判成一手、媒体、聚合、研究、社区五类，构成比例印在报告头上。还没读正文，就知道这份材料有多硬。Every source is classified as primary, media, aggregator, research or community, and the mix is printed at the top of the report. You know how solid the footing is before you read a word.

社区站点的说法会被写明「独立信源强度有限」，而不是和官方披露平起平坐。A community site’s claim is explicitly marked as limited as an independent source, not weighed equally with official disclosures.

常见问题FAQ

一场研究要多久、花多少？How long does a run take, and what does it cost?
视题目而定，通常约 10–15 分钟、三十万 token 上下。上面的「账单」是一次真实运行的完整数字。过程中可以随时离开，回来接着看。It depends on the question — typically ~10–15 minutes and around 300k tokens. The “bill” above is a complete real run. You can leave and come back at any time.
支持哪些模型？Which models are supported?
目前默认走火山方舟 Ark：简单环节用 doubao-seed-2.0-mini，规划与写作用 glm-5.3-flash；仓库里还保留了 MiniMax 和 OpenAI 兼容协议的备用档案。切换 provider 在 config/models.yaml 里完成，但不同平台的协议细节（尤其是「关闭思考」的写法）不同，换之前建议先跑一遍自带的预检脚本。Volcengine Ark by default — doubao-seed-2.0-mini for the light stages, glm-5.3-flash for planning and writing — with MiniMax and OpenAI-compatible fallback profiles kept in the repo. Switching providers is a change in config/models.yaml, but protocols differ (notably how to turn reasoning off) — run the bundled preflight check first.
为什么要我审批框架？Why do I have to approve the outline?
写作是最贵的一步（约占一半 token），也是最难返工的一步。框架先在材料上校准，再让你确认方向，比写完再推倒便宜得多。Writing is the most expensive step (about half the tokens) and the hardest to redo. Calibrating the outline against evidence and letting you confirm direction is far cheaper than rewriting afterwards.
在线体验和自部署有什么区别？Hosted demo vs. self-hosting?
功能相同。在线体验由我们承担模型费用，所以名额有限；自部署用你自己的密钥，数据只在你的机器上。Same features. The hosted demo runs on our model budget, so seats are limited; self-hosting uses your own keys and keeps data on your machine.
